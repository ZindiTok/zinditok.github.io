About ZindîTok

ZindîTok brings live Kurdish television into one place, presented as a vertical feed you can scroll through - swipe between live news, music, culture, and children's channels from across Kurdistan and the diaspora, all streaming in real time.

Tap Follow to keep track of your favorite channels and find them quickly from your Profile. Use the mini player to keep watching while you browse elsewhere, or add ZindîTok to your home screen for one-tap access, just like a native app.

This project is independently built and is not affiliated with any of the broadcasters featured. All streams belong to their respective channels.
